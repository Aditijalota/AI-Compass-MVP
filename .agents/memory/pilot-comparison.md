---
name: Pilot comparison semantics
description: Measured pilot effort versus a frozen preliminary scenario.
---

Keep a pilot's comparison scenario fixed when its observations are first saved, even if that first save is an incomplete draft. Later preliminary assumption edits must not retroactively alter the pilot comparison.

**Why:** Comparing measured results against a moving estimate would obscure whether the original scenario matched actual experience.

**How to apply:** Disclose the capture timing to users. Observations and preliminary assumptions remain separate. Compare only after users confirm matching people, tasks and comparable workload. Adoption is descriptive, not a multiplier of observed effort; zero and negative savings are legitimate outcomes, not validation failures.
