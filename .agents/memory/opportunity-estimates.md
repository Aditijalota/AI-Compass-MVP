---
name: Preliminary opportunity estimates
description: Product meaning of scenario estimates versus pilot readiness.
---

Treat effort-savings scenarios and readiness as separate questions. User confirmation
means the assumptions reflect their current scenario; it is not evidence of measured
savings or permission to implement. Unknown or blocked readiness can coexist with an
effort scenario, and must remain explicit rather than be hidden behind a ranking.

**Why:** Early opportunity comparison must remain useful before pilot prerequisites
are settled, without presenting hypothetical savings as a validated business case.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when adding prioritization or pilot-result
features. Do not turn self-reported readiness into an implied probability of success,
or treat scenario confirmation as a security/governance approval.
