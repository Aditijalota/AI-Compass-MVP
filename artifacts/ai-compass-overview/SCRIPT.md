## Treatment

- Hero: one persistent compass rose / bearing ring that rotates to point at each idea and is carried across every cut.

- Arc: question → three bearings → six-step dialogue → a reasoned verdict with a human gate → a saved map of opportunities → calm lockup.
- Payoff: ending inverts to the ink field; the ring shrinks into the wordmark's "O"-style mark, needle settles north.
